Origins of 4KIDS

From Experience to Independence

The story of 4KIDS Construction Services began with Jolan Baguio, whose nearly 32 years of hands-on experience in waterproofing installation became the foundation of the company.

In 2021, 4KIDS Construction Services was officially registered with the BIR, beginning operations as a duly registered business with waterproofing as its primary service. Together with his wife, Merlita Baguio (Authorized Representative), they continue to build the business upon quality workmanship and integrity.
